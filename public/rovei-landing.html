FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is Rovei booking software? +

No. Rovei focuses on client readiness and client memory. Your booking workflow can stay where it already lives.

Does my client need an account? +

No. The client experience is designed to open from a simple link without requiring an account or app download.

Can I customise what clients see? +

Yes. Studio styling carries through to the client-facing experience, including a Custom colour option.

What are Beauty Packs? +

Reusable client-experience templates for the appointment types you offer most.
